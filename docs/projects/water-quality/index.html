PROJECT 02 / Anomaly Detection · Environment

Water Quality Analysis

Environmental anomaly detection across complex water-quality measurements.

EXPLORE · MODEL · VALIDATE · COMMUNICATE
01

The challenge

Identify unusual environmental readings and latent groupings in multivariate water-quality data.

02

The approach

Combined exploratory analysis with Random Forest, K-Means, and Isolation Forest, using Pandas and SciPy for preparation and statistical investigation.

03

The outcome

A structured analytical view of environmental patterns, clusters, and high-priority anomalies.

04 / LIMITATION

What the project does not solve—yet.

Anomaly labels and geographic coverage are limited, so some unusual readings may reflect sensor error or local conditions rather than true environmental risk.

05 / NEXT ITERATION

How I would move it forward.

Integrate geospatial and time-series context, validate anomalies with domain experts, and support streaming sensor data with real-time alerts.
