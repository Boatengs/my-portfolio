PROJECT 04 / NLP · Deployment

Sentiment Analyzer

A production-facing DistilBERT sentiment model trained on 25,000 reviews.

EXPLORE · MODEL · VALIDATE · COMMUNICATE
01

The challenge

Build an accurate, usable sentiment classifier from a large corpus of movie reviews.

02

The approach

Fine-tuned DistilBERT on 25,000 IMDb reviews and wrapped inference in a lightweight Gradio experience.

03

The outcome

Achieved 91.2% accuracy and deployed a simple interface for real-time sentiment classification.

04 / LIMITATION

What the project does not solve—yet.

Training on IMDb reviews limits generalization to other domains, and the model may struggle with sarcasm, mixed sentiment, or language outside the training distribution.

05 / NEXT ITERATION

How I would move it forward.

Add domain-adaptive and multilingual training, confidence calibration, bias testing, and production monitoring for language drift.
