PROJECT 09 / Computer Vision · Medical Imaging

Skin Lesion Segmentation

Pixel-level lesion segmentation with a U-Net trained from scratch on HAM10000.

LIVE APPLICATION CAPTURE
01

The challenge

Identify the precise boundaries of skin lesions rather than returning only a high-level classification label.

02

The approach

Trained a 31M-parameter U-Net for 20 epochs on 10,015 dermoscopy images, producing a predicted mask and visual overlay.

03

The outcome

Reached a 0.9115 test Dice score and deployed an interactive segmentation workflow on Hugging Face.

04 / LIMITATION

What the project does not solve—yet.

Training on a single public dataset may limit generalization across devices, skin tones, and clinical settings, especially around ambiguous lesion boundaries.

05 / NEXT ITERATION

How I would move it forward.

Use multi-center datasets, stronger augmentation and ensemble methods, uncertainty maps, and clinician-reviewed external validation.
