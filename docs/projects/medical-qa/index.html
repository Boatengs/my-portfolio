PROJECT 06 / LLM Fine-tuning · Research

Medical Q&A Model

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning of Mistral 7B for medical Q&A.

EXPLORE · MODEL · VALIDATE · COMMUNICATE
DATASETMedical Meadow Medical Flashcards
SIZE5,000 fine-tuning examples
SOURCEHugging Face · medalpaca/medical_meadow_medical_flashcards ↗
01

The business problem

Adapt a large language model to domain-specific medical questions without the cost of full fine-tuning.

02

The approach

Fine-tuned Mistral 7B with LoRA/QLoRA using PEFT and TRL, then published the trained adapter to Hugging Face Hub.

03

The outcome

A reproducible parameter-efficient training workflow and shareable domain adapter.

04 / LIMITATION

What the project does not solve—yet.

The adapter was trained on a bounded medical dataset and has not undergone clinician-led safety validation; outputs must not be treated as medical advice.

05 / NEXT ITERATION

How I would move it forward.

Evaluate on larger clinician-reviewed benchmarks, add retrieval grounding and safety filters, and conduct structured human evaluation for factuality and harm.
