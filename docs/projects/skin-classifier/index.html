PROJECT 07 / Computer Vision · Explainability

Skin Disease Classifier

Explainable image classification with transfer learning and Grad-CAM.

EXPLORE · MODEL · VALIDATE · COMMUNICATE
01

The challenge

Classify skin-disease imagery while making model attention more interpretable to users.

02

The approach

Applied EfficientNetB0 transfer learning, added Grad-CAM explanations, and deployed the experience through Gradio.

03

The outcome

An accessible classifier prototype pairing predictions with visual explanation overlays.

04 / LIMITATION

What the project does not solve—yet.

Predictions can be sensitive to image quality and dataset representation, and Grad-CAM indicates attention rather than proving clinical reasoning.

05 / NEXT ITERATION

How I would move it forward.

Train on more diverse clinical imagery, improve calibration and uncertainty reporting, and validate performance with dermatology experts before clinical use.
