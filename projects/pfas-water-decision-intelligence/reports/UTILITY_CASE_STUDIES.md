# Verified Utility Case Studies

## Why these three

| Utility | National rank | Context | Decision stage |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Fayetteville PWC, NC | 5 | Large surface-water utility | GAC selected; major implementation |
| Emmaus Borough, PA | 3 | Municipal groundwater system | Compliance exceedance; remediation / commissioning follow-up |
| Waite Park, MN | 43 | Small groundwater system | Pilot + preliminary engineering |

## Fayetteville PWC, North Carolina

**PWSID NC0326010 — rank 5, priority score 95.8.** UCMR maximum complete-location averages: **PFOA 8.45 ng/L; PFOS 16.85 ng/L**.

PWC reports 98,337 water customers, 225,000+ population served and average demand of 25.07 MGD. PWC states it studied PFAS treatment from October 2021–October 2022 and selected **GAC**. Higher-dose **PAC became operational in March 2026** as an interim measure, with GAC expected in Summer/Fall 2029.

Published context: projected GAC upgrade about **$133M**; secured funding **$84M**; reported remaining need **$49.6M**; anticipated GAC O&M **$10–12M/year**.

**Project assessment:** Continue GAC as the primary long-term path; IX is a benchmark/contingency, whole-flow RO/NF is lower fit at this scale, and PAC is an appropriate bridge rather than endpoint.

## Emmaus Borough, Pennsylvania

**PWSID PA3390032 — rank 3, priority score 99.1.** Four eligible UCMR sampling points triggered; maximum complete-location averages: **PFOA 19.50 ng/L; PFOS 127.00 ng/L**.

Emmaus reports **4,599 customers** and **1.27 MGD** average demand. An official Borough notice reported a Pennsylvania drinking-water violation with **PFOS RAA 103 ng/L** and **PFOA RAA 18 ng/L**, compared with Pennsylvania MCLs of 18 and 14 ng/L.

The Borough's 2023 study compared treat-all-wells, bulk purchase and hybrid strategies. Treating wells had the lowest published 20-year present worth (**$24M–$30M**), with capital **$14.8M–$18.7M** and annual O&M **$0.6M–$1.0M**.

**Project assessment:** Treating the wells remains the strongest published strategy. GAC and IX are both technically credible; verify the installed configuration before choosing between them.

## City of Waite Park, Minnesota

**PWSID MN1730039 — rank 43, priority score 89.4.** UCMR maximum complete-location averages: **PFOA 5.45 ng/L; PFOS 16.50 ng/L**.

Waite Park's 2025 report states that the system uses three groundwater wells. A June 2026 Council agenda report advances a **12–18 month PFAS pilot** plus preliminary engineering. Published predesign costs are **$997,100** for the PFAS pilot, **$440,000** for PFAS preliminary engineering, and **$215,000** for WTP rehabilitation preliminary engineering.

**Project assessment:** Do not force a final GAC-vs-IX choice ahead of the pilot. Both are credible candidates; integrate PFAS work with broader WTP rehabilitation.

## Cross-case conclusion
Fayetteville is primarily an implementation/O&M/funding problem; Emmaus is a commissioning/lifecycle/affordability problem; Waite Park is still a pilot and technology-selection problem. National ranking is therefore only the first step.
