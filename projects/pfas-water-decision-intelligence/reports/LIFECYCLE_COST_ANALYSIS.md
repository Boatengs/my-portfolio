# Lifecycle Cost Scenario Analysis

## Purpose

This model converts **published utility cost ranges** into a transparent 20-year present-value sensitivity analysis. It is a planning model, not a bid estimate.

## Assumptions

- 20-year analysis period
- real discount-rate sensitivity: 2%, 3%, 5%
- capital at Year 0
- published annual O&M treated as a constant real annual cost
- no additional financing cost embedded in lifecycle PV
- no separate media/replacement event unless already represented in published annual O&M
- Waite Park treatment CAPEX remains blank pending pilot/design criteria

## Fayetteville PWC

Published anchors: capital **$133.0M–$146.313M**; annual O&M **$10M–$12M/year**.

At the base midpoint and 3% real discount rate:
- capital: **$139.7M**
- 20-year O&M PV: **$163.7M**
- lifecycle PV: **$303.3M**

Consulting implication: the long-term decision is no longer mainly “which technology?” It is construction cost, media/O&M exposure, residuals, commissioning and financing burden.

## Emmaus Borough

Published treat-all-wells anchors: capital **$14.8M–$18.7M**; annual O&M **$0.6M–$1.0M/year**; published 20-year present worth **$24M–$30M**.

At the base midpoint and 3% real discount rate:
- capital: **$16.75M**
- O&M PV: **$11.90M**
- lifecycle PV: **$28.65M**

The simple project cross-check lands inside the Borough's published range.

## Waite Park

No treatment lifecycle PV is produced yet. Published pilot/preliminary-engineering costs are not treatment construction estimates; assigning a treatment CAPEX before the pilot would create false precision.
