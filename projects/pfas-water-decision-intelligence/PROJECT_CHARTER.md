# Project Charter

## Consulting question

Which public water systems show the strongest evidence of potential PFOA/PFOS treatment need, what treatment pathways should be evaluated, what are the planning-level capital and O&M implications, and how should limited infrastructure funding be prioritized?

## Decision users
- drinking-water utility managers and operators
- consulting engineers and planners
- state drinking-water and infrastructure-funding programs
- capital-planning and asset-management teams

## Workstreams
1. National UCMR 5 evidence base and PFAS screening
2. Transparent engineering-review prioritization
3. Verified utility case studies
4. Treatment and lifecycle-cost analysis
5. Constrained capital allocation
6. Consulting deliverables and reproducibility

## Standards
1. Preserve raw EPA data unchanged.
2. Never label UCMR 5 screening results as regulatory violations.
3. Keep nondetect assumptions explicit.
4. Separate measured evidence, assumptions, modeled quantities and recommendations.
5. Make every priority score decomposable.
6. Present cost estimates as planning-level screening estimates, not final designs or bids.
7. Document uncertainty and sensitivity.
