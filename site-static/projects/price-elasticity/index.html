01

Why this project matters

This capstone began with a practical question for TruRoots and R.W. Knudsen Family: when a product's price changes, how much might customer demand change with it? Answering that question matters because pricing too high can reduce sales, while pricing too low can leave revenue and margin behind.

02

What was developed

Working as part of Capstone Team C, Sam helped turn a heavily redacted promotional retail dataset into an interactive decision support application. The team built nine executive insight views, compared four revenue prediction models, Linear Regression, Ridge, Random Forest, and XGBoost, and added price sensitivity analysis, three segment product clustering, filters, and downloadable results.

03

What it means

Despite substantial confidentiality driven redaction, the team still delivered tangible and decision ready outputs. TruRoots, R.W. Knudsen Family, and participating stakeholders expressed strong satisfaction with the result, particularly the team’s ability to recover useful pricing, promotion, seasonality, and competitive insights from constrained data.

04 / WHERE THIS WORK APPLIES

From project to practical use.

The same idea can support e commerce pricing, consumer packaged goods, grocery and retail promotions, subscription plans, travel pricing, inventory planning, and any business deciding how price changes may influence demand.
