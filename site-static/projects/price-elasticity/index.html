PROJECT 01 / Forecasting · Pricing

Price Elasticity Modeling

Demand and pricing models delivered through an interactive decision dashboard.

EXPLORE · MODEL · VALIDATE · COMMUNICATE
01

The challenge

Translate shifting demand signals into practical pricing guidance while comparing linear and nonlinear forecasting approaches.

02

The approach

Built and compared Random Forest, XGBoost, and ARIMA models, then organized model outputs in a Plotly Dash interface for scenario exploration.

03

The outcome

A reusable modeling workflow that turns historical demand into accessible pricing and forecasting insights.
