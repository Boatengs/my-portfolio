PROJECT 01 / Forecasting · Pricing

Price Elasticity Modeling

A capstone for TruRoots and R.W. Knudsen Family that turned pricing and sales history into a clearer view of how price changes may affect customer demand.

EXPLORE · MODEL · VALIDATE · COMMUNICATE
01

Why this project mattered

This capstone began with a practical question for TruRoots and R.W. Knudsen Family: when a product's price changes, how much might customer demand change with it? Answering that question matters because pricing too high can reduce sales, while pricing too low can leave revenue and margin behind.

02

What I built

I studied historical pricing, promotions, and sales patterns, compared Random Forest, XGBoost, and ARIMA models, and presented the results in an interactive Plotly Dash dashboard. Instead of handing decision-makers raw model output, the dashboard made it easier to explore possible pricing and demand scenarios.

03

What it means

The project showed how past sales behavior can be translated into practical pricing conversations. It gives a business a structured way to compare products, anticipate demand changes, and make pricing decisions with evidence rather than instinct alone.

04 / WHERE THIS WORK APPLIES

From project to practical use.

The same idea can support e-commerce pricing, consumer packaged goods, grocery and retail promotions, subscription plans, travel pricing, inventory planning, and any business deciding how price changes may influence demand.
